About the Movie

When a deep-space listening station picks up a signal that shouldn't exist, a skeleton crew stationed at the edge of the solar system must decide whether to answer it. As communications with Earth fail one by one, trust unravels between the scientists on board, and what began as a routine research mission turns into a fight to understand whether they are truly alone — and whether the signal was ever meant for them.
